How this page was painted.

Color measured, not chosen.

Every color came from five reference works and was measured by two independent algorithms. Shadows and mist fall on a single hue axis, between 271° and 338°.

White on a budget.

In the dark reference paintings, white covers less than 1% of the canvas (0.81% and 0.63%).

Light moves; marble stays.

Five families of motion: mist, current, brushstroke, raking light and ascent. Nothing bounces, nothing spins.

Made in Webflow.

Variables with modes: one altitude per section and per card. GSAP-powered Interactions for text, reflection and mist. A short script for the deck.
